# United Way in Michigan: research for the Connect board and Mentorship tab

Research date: 8 October 2026. Prepared for the Dreamari Connect tab (partner community boards plus Mentorship). Every fact carries its source URL. Anything older than 2024, undated, or not confirmed on an official page is flagged inline as **[older]**, **[undated]** or **[unverified]**.

## Read this first: the map has changed since most people last looked

Four structural changes make many "United Way in Michigan" lists out of date. Do not build the board from an old list.

1. **United Way of Northwest Michigan (Traverse City) ended operations on 30 September 2026.** From 1 October 2026 the Michigan Association of United Ways (MAUW) supports its nine counties (Antrim, Benzie, Charlevoix, Emmet, Grand Traverse, Kalkaska, Leelanau, Missaukee, Wexford) directly, mainly through 2-1-1 and the ALICE report. Contact is nwmi@uwmich.org. Source: https://www.uwmich.org/northwestmi (redirect target of unitedwaynwmi.org).
2. **United Way of Washtenaw County (Ann Arbor) no longer exists as a separate org.** It merged into United Way for Southeastern Michigan (UWSEM) effective 1 October 2023 **[older, but still the current structure]**. UWSEM keeps an Ann Arbor presence. Sources: https://unitedwaysem.org/about-us/uniting-for-a-brighter-future/ and https://www.wemu.org/show/washtenaw-united/2023-10-02/washtenaw-united-united-way-of-washtenaw-county-officially-merges-with-united-way-for-southeastern-michigan
3. **Capital Area United Way (Lansing), United Way of the Battle Creek and Kalamazoo Region, and United Way of Jackson County are one org: United Way of South Central Michigan (UWSCMI, site unitedforscmi.org).** The merger was announced January 2022 and completed April 2022 **[older, still current]**. It covers Calhoun, Clinton, Eaton, Ingham, Jackson and Kalamazoo counties and kept local offices. Sources: https://www.wilx.com/2022/01/25/united-way-jackson-county-merging-with-capital-area-battle-creek-kalamazoo-region and https://www.uwmich.org/our-members-1
4. **The Ottawa/Allegan United Way (Holland) merged into Heart of West Michigan United Way (Grand Rapids) in 2024.** HWMUW now covers Allegan, Kent and Ottawa. Sources: https://www.wgvunews.org/the-shelley-irwin-show/2024-05-09/the-heart-of-west-michigan-united-way-and-the-united-way-of-ottawa-and-allegan-counties-announce-a-regional-collaboration and https://www.hwmuw.org/

Also: "United Way of the Lakeshore" in the brief is the Muskegon org (Muskegon, Newaygo, Oceana). It is not an Ottawa County org.

## 1. Structure: Michigan Association of United Ways (MAUW)

**What it is.** MAUW is the statewide association for Michigan's local United Ways, founded 1947, based at 330 Marshall Street, Suite 211, Lansing. It helps local United Ways collaborate, cut costs and raise revenue, and works with the State of Michigan and statewide nonprofits. It does policy and capacity building. Sources: https://www.uwmich.org/what-we-do and https://www.uwmich.org/

**What it runs.**
- Michigan 2-1-1 (with the Michigan 2-1-1 State Office). https://www.uwmich.org/michigan-211
- The ALICE report for Michigan. https://www.uwmich.org/alice
- Public policy and advocacy, including an annual platform. https://www.uwmich.org/public-policy
- A Community Services Labor Liaison network, which it calls the largest in the nation. https://www.uwmich.org/
- The State Employees Charitable Campaign (SECC). https://www.uwmich.org/what-we-do
- Since 1 October 2026, direct regional support for Northwest Michigan. https://www.uwmich.org/northwestmi

**How many local United Ways.** The official count varies across MAUW's own pages, so the board should say "about 35":
- "Approximately 35" local United Ways in the MAUW network (press release, 5 May 2026). https://www.uwmich.org/pressroom/mauw-amp-mi211-support-state-house-proposed-211-funding-increase
- "36 Local United Ways" (What We Do page, **[undated]**). https://www.uwmich.org/what-we-do
- The Our Members page lists 27 member orgs, and that list still includes Northwest Michigan, which has since closed. https://www.uwmich.org/our-members-1
- An older directory listing says "approximately 60" local United Ways. **[undated, likely older]**. https://michiganvolunteers.org/?p=65018
- Dollars raised: MAUW pages say "over $90 million" (https://www.uwmich.org/what-we-do) and "over $100 million" (https://www.uwmich.org/) a year across the network. Both are **[undated]**.

**MAUW member list (from https://www.uwmich.org/our-members-1, read 8 Oct 2026):** Barry County UW; UW of Bay County (Arenac, Bay, Tuscola); UW of Clare & Gladwin Counties; UW of Delta County; UW of the Eastern Upper Peninsula (Chippewa, Luce, Mackinac); UW of Genesee County (Genesee, Shiawassee); UW of Greater Central Michigan (Gratiot, Ionia, Isabella, Montcalm); Greater Huron County UW; Heart of West Michigan UW (Allegan, Kent, Ottawa); UW of the Lakeshore (Muskegon, Newaygo, Oceana); UW of Lapeer County; Livingston County UW; UW of Marquette County; Mecosta-Osceola UW; UW of Midland County; UW of Monroe/Lenawee Counties; UW of Northeast Michigan (Alcona, Alpena, Montmorency); UW of Northwest Michigan (closed 30 Sept 2026); UW of Otsego County; Plymouth Community UW; UW of Saginaw County; UW of South Central Michigan (Calhoun, Clinton, Eaton, Ingham, Jackson, Kalamazoo); UW for Southeastern Michigan (Macomb, Oakland, Washtenaw, Wayne); UW of Southwest Michigan (Berrien, Cass, Van Buren); UW of St. Clair County; St. Joseph County UW; UW of the West Shore (Mason, Manistee).

### ALICE in Michigan

There are two recent editions. **Use the 2026 edition (2024 data) as the headline.**

**2026 update (released June 2026, 2024 data).** PDF: https://unitedwaysem.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/state-of-alice-report-michigan-2026.pdf (also at https://unitedforscmi.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/state-of-alice-report-michigan-2026.pdf)
- 4,109,904 total households in Michigan.
- 551,257 (13.4%) were in poverty, and 1,079,772 (26.3%) were ALICE. Together that is **1,631,029 households (39.7%, rounded to 40%) below the ALICE Threshold**.
- Household Survival Budget: **$29,580 for a single adult** ($14.79/hour full time) and **$78,216 for a family of four** with two adults, an infant and a preschooler ($39.11/hour).
- Youth-relevant: **67% of households headed by someone under 25** were below the threshold, the highest of any age group.
- 73% of single-female-headed families with children were below the threshold.
- By county, the rate was highest in Clare (55%) and lowest in Livingston (29%). In Wayne County it ranged by ZIP from 75% (48204, Detroit) to 13% (48168, Northville).
- Below-threshold households rose from 1,516,306 to 1,631,029 over the report's trend period.

**2025 edition (released May 2025, 2023 data).** https://www.uwmich.org/alice (PDF named State-of-ALICE-Michigan-Report-2025.pdf)
- 41% of Michigan households were below the ALICE Threshold: 14% in poverty plus 27% ALICE. Source for the split: https://connect.unitedforscmi.org/en/public/blogs/the-state-of-alice-in-our-region-2025-09-01
- The uwmich.org ALICE page headline says "1.6 Million Households." This matches the 2026 figure, so the page may already be updated. **[unverified which edition the headline refers to]**

**Regional ALICE figures for local boards.**
- UWSEM four-county region (Macomb, Oakland, Washtenaw, Wayne): 42%, per the FY2024-25 Annual Report. https://unitedwaysem.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/090-2026-8.5x11-2024-25-Annual-Report-V1-copy.pdf
- UWSCMI six-county region: 41% (about 171,521 households; 2025 edition). City rates: Jackson 64%, Kalamazoo 53%, Battle Creek 51%, Lansing 50%. https://connect.unitedforscmi.org/en/public/blogs/the-state-of-alice-in-our-region-2025-09-01
- HWMUW (Allegan, Kent, Ottawa): "1 in 3 households" struggle to meet basic needs. https://www.hwmuw.org/

### Michigan 2-1-1

- Free, confidential, 24/7. Dial 2-1-1 or 844-875-9211, text your ZIP code to 898211, or search https://mi211.org. Source: https://www.uwmich.org/michigan-211
- **FY2025 (1 Oct 2024 to 30 Sep 2025): 625,852 requests, up 8% from 578,446 in FY2024. 400,704 documented connections, 93% of them by phone. 693,433 referrals. 250,596 unique web searches, up 25%.** Top reasons were housing (26%), utility assistance (21%), information services (18%), food (6%) and income support (6%). Unmet need was 14%. The database lists more than 27,000 programs. Source: https://mi211.org/wp-content/uploads/FY25-MDHHS-ISD-Annual-Report-final.pdf
- There are seven regional contact centers: 2-1-1 Northeast Michigan; CALL 2-1-1 (Community Access Line of the Lakeshore); Central Michigan 2-1-1; Gryphon Place 2-1-1; Heart of West Michigan United Way 2-1-1; UPCAP 2-1-1; and United Way for Southeastern Michigan 2-1-1. Same source.
- Funding: the Michigan House proposed FY27 budget adds $1 million, bringing 2-1-1 to $3.95 million total. MAUW backed it on 5 May 2026. https://www.uwmich.org/pressroom/mauw-amp-mi211-support-state-house-proposed-211-funding-increase
- Center-level numbers:
  - UWSEM 2-1-1 had 153,421 calls (FY2024-25). https://unitedwaysem.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/090-2026-8.5x11-2024-25-Annual-Report-V1-copy.pdf
  - UWSEM's 2025 Donor Impact Report says 274,640 people got help through 2-1-1 (a different measure from calls). https://unitedwaysem.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2025-Donor-Impact-Report.pdf
  - HWMUW 2-1-1 answered 47,485 calls across 15 counties (2024-25). https://www.hwmuw.org/impact-reports/2024-25-impact-report
  - Genesee County had 19,985 calls. https://www.unitedwaygenesee.org/ **[undated]**
- February is 2-1-1 Awareness Month in Michigan (WEMU, February 2025). https://www.wemu.org/show/washtenaw-united/2025-02-10/washtenaw-united-february-is-2-1-1-awareness-month

- The latest platform shown on uwmich.org is "2024 Public Policy Priorities" **[2024]**: expand the state EITC, support 2-1-1, raise postsecondary attainment for working adults (70% of Michigan jobs need some postsecondary education), make child care accessible, and expand affordable housing. https://www.uwmich.org/public-policy

### 2.1 United Way for Southeastern Michigan (UWSEM)

- **HQ:** Detroit. The Fisher Building, 3011 W Grand Blvd, is listed at https://michiganvolunteers.org/listing-item/united-way-for-southeastern-michigan/ **[verify address]**. Ann Arbor office kept after the Washtenaw merger.
- **Counties:** Macomb, Oakland, Washtenaw, Wayne (4). https://unitedwaysem.org/
- **Website:** https://unitedwaysem.org
- **Scale:**
  - FY2024-25 community impact spending was $40,534,411. Total community investment including Ballmer Group summer funding was $104,634,957. Annual campaign revenue was $25,266,852. https://unitedwaysem.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/090-2026-8.5x11-2024-25-Annual-Report-V1-copy.pdf
  - The 2025 Donor Impact Report says "more than $51 million" invested last year, 197 local organizations funded, and 837,692 people reached. https://unitedwaysem.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2025-Donor-Impact-Report.pdf

**Youth programs (verified current):**
- **DiscoverWorks (formerly Summer Discovery).** Free, full-day summer learning for rising K through rising 10th grade, with half-day options for rising 9th and 10th graders. It runs at least 4 to 5 weeks at host sites, funded by Ballmer Group.

  - Sources: https://unitedwaysem.org/resources/discoverworks/ , https://unitedwaysem.org/resources/summer-discovery/how-it-works , and the FY2024-25 Annual Report.

- **Youth Skilled Trades Education.** Named as a beneficiary of the labor team's Sneaker Ball (May 2025, $26,000). There is no program page, so details are **[unverified]**. FY2024-25 Annual Report.
- **Books and backpacks.**
  - 33,830 books to 6,766 children through My Home Library (Donor Impact Report).
  - 6,875 backpacks packed by 188 volunteers from 15 corporate partners in August 2025 (Annual Report).

## What this means for the board

1. **Lead with the statewide frame, not a single org.**
   - "About 35 local United Ways" (MAUW, May 2026).
   - "40% of Michigan households (1.63 million) are below the ALICE Threshold."
   - "**67% of households headed by someone under 25** are below it." This is the line that lands with teens.
   - "2-1-1 handled 625,852 requests in FY2025."
2. **Do not show Northwest Michigan as an active United Way.** Do not list Capital Area, Battle Creek/Kalamazoo, Jackson or Washtenaw as separate orgs. Pins should be the merged orgs: UWSEM (Detroit, with Ann Arbor), UWSCMI (Lansing, Battle Creek, Kalamazoo and Jackson offices), and HWMUW (Grand Rapids, with Holland and Allegan).
3. **The strongest real youth stories to feature:**
   - HWMUW **Student United Way** (high school, current, with numbers).
   - Lakeshore's **career pathways** (Jobs for Michigan's Graduates, Summer Work Experience, MiCareerQuest, College Exposure; 892 students in 2025).
   - UWSEM **DiscoverWorks** (rising 9th and 10th grade options) and its funded career mentor partner **Winning Futures**.
   - **Days of Caring** that welcome school groups (Lakeshore, Bay, HWMUW).
4. **Mentorship tab.**
   - The honest model is "United Way funds mentoring partners." Big Brothers Big Sisters affiliates are the common thread statewide, and Winning Futures is the closest match to career mentoring for high schoolers.
   - Any in-app e-mentoring would be a Dreamari feature, not a replica of a Michigan United Way program. Seed demo copy should say "funded by" or "partner of," not "run by," and should be flagged DEMO-ONLY.
5. **Do not present the historical Detroit career work as live** (Find Your Future fair, College and Career Pathways, NAF lab, United Way-presented GDYT fairs). GDYT is real and current, but it is the City of Detroit's program (DESC and Connect Detroit). It can appear only as a "related opportunity."
6. **Events.** Few teen-facing United Way events between October 2026 and spring 2027 have published dates. Use real dated items (Marquette volunteer shifts, Power of the Purse, Women of Influence, Housing Kalamazoo) plus clearly labeled "usually in [month]" items (MLK Day 18 Jan 2027, Days of Caring, Youth Day of Caring in May, GDYT applications in March to May). Demo dates beyond that should be flagged DEMO-ONLY.
