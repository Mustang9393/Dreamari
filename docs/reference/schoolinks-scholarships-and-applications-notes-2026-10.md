# SchooLinks: Scholarship Matching, College Financing and the College Application Manager

Watched 1 Oct 2026 for the Opportunities tab (Joshua's thread, 30 Sept: "where do we put scholarships and internships"). Two public walkthrough videos, transcripts pulled and key frames stepped through:

- Scholarships: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KQDK3LhmzU8 (Elizabeth Emmons, Milford, 5:07, Oct 2025)
- College Application Manager: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cKpGVDmGsO4 (St. Mary's County Public Schools, 8:04, Oct 2025)

## Scholarship Matching (College > Scholarship Matching)

Screen anatomy:
- Three edit cards across the top: Eligibility, Interests, Colleges. Eligibility is a questionnaire (gender, ZIP, race/ethnicity, origins/ancestry, religion, then GPA weighted/unweighted and more). Interests and Colleges are picked separately. Matches are computed from the three.
- Four views as a pill group, top right: Matches (default), Favorites, District Posted, Search. "1154 Scholarships", sorted by Best Match (dropdown).
- Master/detail: list on the left, detail pane on the right ("Click on a scholarship to view details").
- List row: a small "Posted by your district" eyebrow when the school posted it; name; provider; deadline as a date (Feb 4, Dec 1); five dollar icons as an amount rating; a match ring with a percentage (100, 88).
- Detail pane: name and provider; three actions on one line: Favorite (heart), Mark as applied (clipboard), Apply (primary, external). Then Deadline, Amount, Annual winners as a three-column fact row, then Description. District postings carry a yellow disclaimer: added by your district, not verified by SchooLinks.
- Apply always leaves the platform: "if you click that apply button, it'll take you to that application."

Counselor advice in the video (worth turning into student copy):
- "Typically your higher dollar scholarships, the scholarships you're most likely to receive, are going to come from your universities." Check each school's financial aid page; ask about honors programs, living-learning communities, major-specific scholarships, merit aid for your profile.
- "You almost want to be applying to scholarships like it's a part-time job."
- "Anything that requires your credit card is not a scholarship site you want to be on."
- Out-of-state merit examples: Kentucky and Alabama offering housing and tuition at 3.8 GPA / 33 ACT. Private schools discount heavily.
- Students can also use large sites such as Going Merry.

## College Financing (College > College Financing)

- FAFSA status with an "update status" and confirmation upload.
- Financial background entry, third-party scholarships ("add scholarships awarded to you that can be used at any college").
- Per college, over four years: Net cost, split into Out of pocket, Borrow amount, Gift aid, as a three-colour bar. Estimated until a school is accepted in the application manager, then actual.

## College Application Manager (College > College applications)

- Setup first: home address, email, fee-waiver eligibility, FERPA waiver signature.
- Board view with status columns: Incomplete, Completed and pending, Accepted, plus Denied, Will not complete, Withdrawn along the bottom. List view alternative, sortable by status, with due dates.
- Add application from the Final list (or search). Per application: application method and type, document destination (in-network = electronic), student checklist (required items pre-checked), teacher recommendations (search teacher, or add manually from another district), counselor documents (transcript and counselor recommendation auto-requested on save).
- Left rail: Recommendation tracker (Fulfilled / Pending / Declined), FAFSA (Not filed, Update status), Common App (Connected / Log in), Application setup (Personal details, Fee waiver eligibility, FERPA waiver).
- Results: accepted, denied, waitlisted, deferred, withdrawn. Applications are never submitted here; they go through Common App, Coalition, QuestBridge or the college's own site.

## What Dreamari takes, and what it leaves

Takes:
- Match first. The list opens on what the student already qualifies for; no empty search box.
- One row anatomy: name, who gives it, amount, closes-on date, one line of why it fits.
- Three actions on the detail: Save, Mark as applied, Apply on [source] (external, labelled, per handoff rule 21).
- "Posted by" eyebrow for partner postings (SchooLinks: "Posted by your district"; Dreamari: the Connect partner org), with a short trust note.
- The advice as copy: schools give the most money (link to the student's saved schools), never pay to apply.
- FAFSA as a status the student updates, read by the counselor's Financial Aid screen (v3).

Leaves:
- The upfront questionnaire (gender, race, religion). Dreamari matches from what the profile already knows (grade, state, GPA, Top 3 careers, saved schools) and asks nothing extra to start.
- The percentage match ring and dollar rating. A percentage invents precision the data does not have; the amount is shown as a number and the fit as reasons.
- The application manager. College applications stay a My Plan step in grade 12 and the counselor's v3 Applications screen; Opportunities holds scholarships and programs only.
