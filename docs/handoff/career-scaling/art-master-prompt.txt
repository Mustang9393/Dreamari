LIGHT
Use the same key light, fill colour and shadow direction on people, furniture and room. Express light on people through broad cel shapes, not extra drawing detail.
Dawn: pale low warm light, cool room fill. Day: clear neutral daylight and blue sky. Late afternoon: lower warmer sun with cooler unlit areas. Night: a dark exterior and believable practical lamps.
Soft god rays are optional. Use them only where the window, sun angle and air support them: faint, broad, soft-edged shafts fading into clear air. No fog across faces, glitter, rigid light stripes or daylight rays at night. Do not add sunset merely to make a frame attractive.
