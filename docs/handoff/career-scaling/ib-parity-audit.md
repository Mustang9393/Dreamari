# Investment Banking source audit and Level 1 adaptation

22 October 2026. Primary source: `src/components/play/ib-level-1-v2.ts`, with relationship continuity checked against `ib-level-2.ts` and `ib-level-3.ts`.

The former UX/Food scripts matched IB's 29-beat count while changing its intentional structure. They opened on the mentor, taught three vocabulary terms, scored matching instead of treating it as practice, moved the timed round late and reduced it to three questions. Their colleagues were mostly task helpers; they lacked Jordan's early seed, later credit conflict and continuing rivalry. Those were substantive departures, not cosmetic differences.

| IB function | Required adapted function |
| --- | --- |
| Workplace welcome, then scarce return-offer stakes | Workplace and prestigious project first; credible next-assignment stakes. No guaranteed job or instant promotion. |
| Christina introduction and hierarchy | Mentor appears at slot 3, then explains authority and review channel. |
| Jordan at reception | Brief peer encounter at slot 5; do not announce a villain or list the cast. |
| Four word cards | Four career terms, one at a time. |
| Four phrase-to-action matches | Apply those exact terms; unscored, no verdict, no reputation change. |
| Four timed professional checks | Four questions, 45 seconds, applying the taught career tools or methods. |
| Rule, then applied decision | Teach a career method, then apply it to the actual assignment; storage is not mandatory. |
| Marcus arrival and authority | Senior evaluator enters for the client review, then establishes evidence standard and authority. |
| Senior work check | Perform or critique a concrete job task: a neutral UX test prompt or a food sample-record check. |
| Midpoint after four scored decisions | Threshold 68; resume into the second act. |
| Jordan returns, then claims the player's spreadsheet | The same established peer takes credit for the player's work; the player has a file history. |
| Private response choice | Protect credit using evidence and the mentor channel, rather than public conflict. |
| Workload shock, deadline, mentor message | Flag the gap and request priorities before the deadline. |
| Delivery followed by another person's urgent work | Player has capacity; rank concrete help above empty sympathy or mockery. |
| Christina notices; final review; return offer | Mentor notices teamwork. Earn a next assignment; retain relationships for planned future chapters. |

UX uses Arjun as mentor, Noah as the recurring peer rival, Samira as evaluator and Amina as the late colleague needing help. Food uses Ren, Imani, Yuki and Luis in those same narrative functions. The technical evidence remains career-specific: observed usability behavior in UX; a controlled ingredient trial in Food.

The adapted continuity ledgers retain names, roles, rival motivation, the credit incident and unresolved next-project tension. `ib-slot-adaptations.json` records all 29 roles for each adaptation. Introduction ledgers, teach/apply traces and continuity ledgers are separate inspectable artifacts. Tests compare story functions, practice/scored positions, counts, scoring, first appearances and prior teaching. Technical question layouts follow researched task contracts rather than IB layout equality. These checks catch structural drift; they do not certify all narrative quality or every runtime branch.
