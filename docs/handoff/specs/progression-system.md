# Progression system: Dreams, Your Sky, Altitude, badges

Status: Proposal for review with Joshua, 6 September 2026. Not locked. The only implemented piece is the Build milestone (+100 XP) described in dream-score.md. Every threshold below is a pilot setting to validate, not a measured result. Nothing here should be built until Joshua signs off on the four decisions in the deck (Dreamari-Dream-Score-for-Josh.pptx, kept outside the repo because of its size).

Visuals for this spec live in `assets/progression/`: `badge-sheet.jpg` (every badge shape, tier and family), `sky.png`, `dreams.png`, `altitude.png`.

## Contract (unchanged)
Every reward points to something the student learned or did. Dream Score only goes up. Views, swipes, likes, follows, logins, shares, GPA, income and prestige earn nothing. "Not for me" earns exactly the same as "yes". Dream Score never means employable and is never shown to partners as a ranking.

## Grounding
- Savickas' career adaptability resources: concern, control, curiosity, confidence. Each badge family develops one resource and has a product metric.
- Super's exploration tasks (crystallise, specify, implement) are the arc of a Dream's states.
- Stumpf, Colarelli and Hartman: self exploration and environment exploration are separate, so reflections earn as much as facts.
- Possible selves (Markus and Nurius): the collectible is a career the student might become, and a ruled-out possible self is kept, not deleted.
- Hanus and Fox (2015): no leaderboards, no mandatory competition. Sailer and Homner (2020): learning gains are real, motivation gains depend on design.
- Tiered and limited-edition badge precedents: Pokémon GO medals, Duolingo achievements, Khan Academy tiers, Apple Watch limited editions, Strava challenge finishers.

## Dreams
A Dream is a career the student is seriously considering. Every career saved from Explore or Match becomes a Dream card.

States, moved only by evidence, never backwards:
1. Spark: saved with an insight.
2. Tried: a simulation chapter or an event stub attached.
3. Weighed: a comparison with a written trade-off against another Dream.
4. Known: shelved with a written reason after a real try (Tried or Weighed first). Kept and shown, dimmer, forever. Counts as progress and earns 20 XP.
5. Chosen: saved as #1 with My Plan active.

The Top 3 is a view: the three brightest Dreams. Changing #1 re-orders the Sky and never deletes history.

## Your Sky
Profile tab. Each Dream is a star. Brightness is evidence count. A ring marks the #1. Dashed lines connect Dreams the student compared. Known Dreams are faint violet with a dashed ring. Worlds are constellations (ten regions for the ten career worlds). Exports as a still image to the Scrapbook or the phone. Wrapped ends on the Sky, animated.

| Milestone (product verb) | Badge | Evidence | Unlocks |

| Built | Foundation | Profile finished (live, 100 XP) | Your Sky opens with the first Sparks |
| Matched | First Light | Top 3 saved with one reason for the #1 | My Plan for the #1 |
| Played | Clocked In | One chapter plus its reflection | Dreamy outfit from that career |

Career emblems: one ID-card badge per simulation, earned by finishing the career's full simulation with reflections. Not tiered. Each unlocks a Dreamy outfit and a one-week title. Today: The Analyst (Investment Banking, Cobalt Capital), The Nurse (Registered Nurse, Riverbend), The Pilot, The Engineer, The Scientist (Food Scientist), The Doctor (Emergency Medicine), The Accountant, The Mechanic (Aviation Maintenance), The Investor (Private Equity).

Editions: ticket-shaped event editions (stub plus one follow-up action within seven days), season pennants (six week stamps in a semester). Never removed once earned.

Secret badges (starburst, hidden until earned): Full Circle (return to a Known Dream), Plot Twist (Top 3 fully replaced in one semester after real tries), Long Way Round (five worlds before choosing), Second Opinion (the same question to two professionals), Both Sides (Analyst and Associate levels at Cobalt Capital).

## Badge design system
One meaning per channel.
- Shape: hexagon milestone, circle family badge, ID card career emblem, ticket event edition, pennant season, starburst secret.
- Ring: Spark thin dusty blue; Glow double lavender; Beam gold with twelve rays; Aurora three-colour ring (brand blue, violet, blush) with glow, animated in app, static in exports.
- Disc colour: family (table above), all existing semantic tokens.
- Glyph: lucide icons already used by the app (compass, briefcase-business, scale, list-checks, messages-square, languages, and one per emblem).
- States: locked greyed with lock and name; earned full colour; secret shows a starburst with "???" until earned; new badge uses the 1.1s slot-in of the score chip.
- Sizes: 24 px chip, 48 px badge case, 96 px detail sheet with evidence, 320 px Scrapbook and share card.

## Stubs (events only)
Check in by QR or host confirmation. Write one takeaway within seven days to keep the stub; an unwritten stub fades. Stubs live in the Scrapbook, count toward In the Room, unlock the event edition with one follow-up action, and export into the Verified Experience Record. Chapters, words and questions are not stubs; they have their own badges.

## Rhythm
- First-loop quest on Home: Build (done) → Match → Play chapter 1 → save an insight → ask one question.
- This week's three: three tasks from My Plan; finishing all three earns a week stamp. Weeks stamped are counted; a missed week costs nothing.
- Episode drops: a new chapter for the #1 career each Thursday.
- Still true? check 30 days after saving a Top 3; either answer earns 20 XP.
- Future You letter written at Build, delivered with Wrapped.
- Seasons: Fall and Spring, each closing with Wrapped (five cards ending on Your Sky).

## Rewards
Cosmetic: Dreamy wardrobe (one outfit per emblem), Sky themes (Altitude, and a Beam in any family unlocks that family's theme), titles for a week. Eligibility, never XP: breakout seats for students who finished the related simulation, shadow-day places by lottery among Conversation Ready students, partner Open Doors weeks. Dreamy never sulks after a break.

## Rejected
Leaderboards and class rankings; XP decay, loss streaks, sad Dreamy; loot boxes, random rewards, XP purchases; XP or payment for priority answers; XP for shares, follows or invites; auto-posting to social media.
