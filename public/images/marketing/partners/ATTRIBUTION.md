# Partner marks

Vector marks of Dream Opportunity's corporate partners, used as monochrome silhouettes in the landing pages' partner ticker. Each file is trimmed to its drawn pixels (the SVG viewBox is the ink bounding box) so the ticker can scale every mark by pixels, not by its original frame.

Sources: Wikimedia Commons (Special:FilePath) for Verizon, Yahoo, Nielsen, Colgate, Versace, Michael Kors, VH1, BET, DC, TNT, Warner Bros. Discovery, Informa, Kellanova, Chase; the repo's own `public/images/logos/companies` set for JPMorgan Chase, Goldman Sachs, Kellogg's, EY, HSBC, Blackstone, Amazon, AT&T. Nickelodeon is left out: its splat and text merge as a silhouette. Logos are trademarks of their owners and appear here to identify Dream Opportunity's partners. Pringles has no vector on Commons and is not included yet; MTV's available vector is a filled brand block that does not read as a silhouette, so it is left out.

8 Sept 2026 pass, also via Wikimedia Commons Special:FilePath: Kroll (`File:Kroll logo (2021).svg`), Brookfield (`File:Brookfield_logo.svg`), Akamai (`File:Akamai_logo.svg`), Paramount (`File:Paramount-Mountain-Logo.svg`), WildBrain (`File:WildBrain_logo.svg`), Bleacher Report (`File:Bleacher_Report_logo.svg`, a black square with a painted-white "R" -- needs the luminance-invert treatment like HSBC and Warner Bros. Discovery, since flattening it to flat white collapses the whole badge to a blank block), Cartoon Network (`File:Cartoon Network 2010 logo.svg`, the black/white two-square "CN" mark; the reference wall shows it as a rotated diamond badge, but Commons only has the unrotated square presentation of the same real mark, so it is used as-is rather than applying a rotation not in the source file -- also needs luminance-invert for the same reason as Bleacher Report), GDC (`File:Game_Developers_Conference_logo.svg`), Peloton (`File:Peloton (Unternehmen) logo.svg`), Jimmy Choo (`File:Jimmy Choo wordmark.svg`, the plain wordmark, not the `File:Jimmy_Choo_logo.svg` "JC" monogram which doesn't match the wall's plain-text mark). Pop-Tarts has no vector on Commons; the only asset (`File:Poptarts brand logo.png`) is a raster with a baked-in blue outline that collapses into a solid blob under the flat white treatment used for plain marks, so it uses the luminance-invert treatment instead (verified by rendering both treatments) and is included as `pop-tarts.png`, following the `att.png` raster precedent.

Not sourced (checked against Wikimedia Commons, 8 Sept 2026): MRO (Aviation Week MRO), BioProcess International, SupplySide Global, Natural Products Expo West, Brand Licensing Europe, IWCE, MAGIC (Las Vegas trade show), MD&M, enterprise CONNECT, The AI Summit London, NCSolutions -- all B2B/trade-show brands with no Commons page found. Taylor & Francis -- Commons only has a historic oil-lamp emblem (`File:Taylor and Francis.svg`); the wall shows the current ship-in-circle "Taylor & Francis Group" mark, which isn't on Commons as a vector. McDermott Will & Schulte -- the wall's transcribed name doesn't match any real law firm (the real firm is McDermott Will & Emery), so no logo was sourced under either name to avoid misattribution. The black "[A/B]" bracket mark and the mustache-face mark (both unlabeled icon-only logos next to the trade-show cluster) could not be confidently identified to a specific real trademark from the image alone, so neither was sourced. (The mustache-face mark was later identified as Pringles' "Julius Pringles" emblem -- see the second pass below; the "[A/B]" bracket remains unidentified.)

Second pass (8 Sept 2026, direct feedback: "these are our partners... we already use them on the Dream Opportunity website" -- most of the above turned out to be directly fetchable from each brand's own official site, not just Commons): sourced directly from official sites via each event/brand's own hosted logo asset (all served from Informa's shared `knect365.imgix.net` CDN, since Informa owns/operates these event brands) -- BioProcess International, SupplySide Global, MRO (from aviationweek.com), Natural Products Expo West, Brand Licensing Europe, IWCE, MD&M, enterprise CONNECT (as "Enterprise Connect"), The AI Summit London (cropped to drop the dated "Celebrating 10 Years" badge, keeping the evergreen wordmark). Pringles sourced from Wikimedia Commons (`File:Pringles_2021.svg`, the current Julius Pringles mark) -- this is also the "mustache-face" mark from the first pass's unidentified list.

Still not sourced after the second pass: MAGIC and Taylor & Francis's official sites both returned 403 Forbidden on direct fetch (bot-blocked); NCSolutions has been absorbed into Circana and no longer has a standalone current logo, only a footer text mention ("NCSolutions is now part of Circana") -- the wall's mark is presumably NCSolutions' pre-acquisition logo, which is not straightforwardly available anymore. McDermott Will & Schulte and the "[A/B]" bracket icon remain unidentified for the same reasons as the first pass.
