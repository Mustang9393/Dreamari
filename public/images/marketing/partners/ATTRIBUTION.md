# Partner marks

Vector marks of Dream Opportunity's corporate partners, used as monochrome silhouettes in the landing pages' partner ticker. Each file is trimmed to its drawn pixels (the SVG viewBox is the ink bounding box) so the ticker can scale every mark by pixels, not by its original frame.

Sources: Wikimedia Commons (Special:FilePath) for Verizon, Yahoo, Nielsen, Colgate, Versace, Michael Kors, Nickelodeon, VH1, BET, DC, TNT, Warner Bros. Discovery, Informa, Kellanova, Chase; the repo's own `public/images/logos/companies` set for JPMorgan Chase, Kellogg's, EY, HSBC, Blackstone, Amazon, AT&T. Logos are trademarks of their owners and appear here to identify Dream Opportunity's partners. Pringles has no vector on Commons and is not included yet; MTV's available vector is a filled brand block that does not read as a silhouette, so it is left out.
