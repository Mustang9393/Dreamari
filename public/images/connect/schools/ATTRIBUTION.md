# School marks

Seals and wordmarks for the schools named in Connect professionals' `education`
field (src/components/connect/data.ts), shown as small logo tiles on a
professional's profile (About card) instead of a bare GraduationCap icon.
Same method as the corporate partner marks
(public/images/marketing/partners/ATTRIBUTION.md): sourced via Wikimedia
Commons' Special:FilePath (or, for a mark that is non-free/trademarked and
therefore hosted locally rather than on Commons, English Wikipedia's own file
page, fetched the same way), each file trimmed to its own ink bounding box
(the SVG viewBox rewritten from a rendered alpha scan; raster files cropped to
their alpha bounds) so a tile can size a mark by its own pixels, not its
original canvas. Logos are trademarks of their institutions and appear here
only to identify where a professional studied.

## Not included

- **School of Motion**: an online design-education brand, not an accredited
  university -- has no seal or wordmark on Wikimedia Commons or Wikipedia to
  source, and one shouldn't be invented. `pro-fontaine`'s About card keeps the
  plain GraduationCap icon for this line.
- **Art Center College of Design**: the only mark found on Commons is
  `ArtCenter dot large RGB.png`, an abstract solid orange circle used in the
  school's identity system ("the dot") -- on its own, at a small icon size, it
  doesn't read as identifying the school (it could be any circle), so it's
  left out rather than passed off as a logo. `pro-johnson`'s About card keeps
  the plain GraduationCap icon for this line.
